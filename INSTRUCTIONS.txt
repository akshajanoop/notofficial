2. CUSTOMER GUIDE: BOOKING TICKETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Browse Movies
  • Navigate to the Home page (/) to explore currently running movies.
  • Filter movies by genre or select recommendations personalized for your city.
  • Click "Book Tickets" on any movie card to view available cinema locations and showtimes.

Step 2: Choose Theatre & Showtime
  • Select a cinema hall and pick your desired date and time slot.
  • Click on the showtime button to open the auditorium seat selection page.

Step 3: Visual Auditorium Seat Map
  • The auditorium is arranged with 3 seating tiers:
    - Standard (Rows A–C): Direct close-up view with high visual immersion.
    - Premium (Rows D–E): Center sweet spot with eye-level screen alignment and optimal Dolby Atmos audio.
    - Recliner (Row F): VIP motorized luxury plush recliners with panoramic screen view.
  • Click any green (available) seat to select it. Click again to unselect.
  • The order sidebar dynamically calculates the total price.
  • Click "Proceed to Checkout" to temporarily lock your selected seats for 5 minutes.

Step 4: Checkout & Loyalty Points Redemption
  • View your seat breakdown and order total.
  • Loyalty Credits:
    - Every booking earns 10% cashback credits automatically upon completion.
    - 1 Loyalty Point = ₹1.00 instant discount.
    - Enter the number of points to redeem (or click "Max") to discount your total.
  • Click "Pay & Confirm Reservation" to finalize the booking.
  • On the confirmation screen, you receive your booking reference ID (#CR-XXXX).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LIVE TICKET AUCTION & RESALE EXCHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CineReserve features a live bidding exchange where seats can be auctioned in real time.

• Participating in an Active Auction:
  1. Open "Live Ticket Auctions & Resale" in the top navigation (/auctions).
  2. Select an active auction room to view the countdown timer and current bid.
  3. Enter an amount higher than the current highest bid and click "Place Bid".
  4. The screen auto-refreshes every 3 seconds with live synchronized updates.
  5. When the countdown timer expires, the highest bidder wins the ticket.

• Listing Any Seat for Auction:
  1. Customers can list seats they cannot attend from their profile page.
  2. Anyone can also list available cinema seats from the right panel of the Auctions page.
  3. Choose the show and seat, set a starting minimum bid (e.g., ₹250), and select the auction duration.
  4. The auction immediately becomes public and accessible to all users.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Access your profile by clicking your name in the top navigation bar (/profile).

• Click "Auction Ticket" on any confirmed booking to resell or auction a seat.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ADMINISTRATOR PORTAL GUIDE (/admin)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Accessing the Portal:
  Log in as admin@cinereserve.com / admin123. The top navigation bar will show
  an "Admin Portal" link. Any attempt to access profile or settings redirects
  to the Admin Control Center.

• Tab 1: Movies Management
  - Add Movie: Enter title, genre, rating, duration, poster image URL, director,
    and cast/crew members. Click "Add Movie to Catalog".
  - Edit Movie: Click "Edit" on any movie in the table to modify its title,
    rating, poster URL, or details in a modal dialog.
  - Delete Movie: Click "Delete". All dependent shows, seats, auctions, and bookings
    are safely cascaded and removed cleanly without database constraint errors.

• Tab 2: Theatres Management
  - Add Theatre: Enter the cinema hall name, street address, city, and GPS coordinates.
  - Edit/Delete Theatre: Update address details or remove outdated cinema locations.

• Tab 3: Show Scheduling
  - Add Show: Link a movie to a cinema hall, select the screening date and time.
    30 auditorium seats (Standard, Premium, and VIP Recliners) are automatically generated.
  - Edit/Delete Show: Modify showtimes or remove cancelled screenings.

• Tab 4: Customer Bookings Management
  - View all user reservations across all movies and cinemas in real time.
  - Modify Status: Change a booking's status to CONFIRMED, CANCELLED, REFUNDED, or PENDING.
  - Cancelling or Refunding: Setting status to CANCELLED or REFUNDED automatically releases
    the reserved seats back to the public pool so other moviegoers can book them.
  - Delete Booking: Permanently removes the booking record and frees up seats.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA PERSISTENCE & DATABASE STORAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• The application uses a persistent file-based H2 database stored at:
  ./data/movie_booking_db.mv.db
• All admin additions, edits, deletions, and customer bookings are permanently saved
  to disk and persist across application restarts, re-runs, and recompilations.
• To completely reset the database to sample seed data, simply stop the app and
  delete the ./data directory.
